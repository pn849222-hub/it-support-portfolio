When I collected 672 job postings from ITviec, every salary came labelled as US dollars. 24 of them were really in Vietnamese dong:

Averaging those as dollars would have mixed 14 (million dong) with 1,500 (dollars). My pipeline reads the currency from the text instead of trusting the label. That fix and three others are in the case study.

Vietnam IT job market

I collected 672 live postings from 320 companies, cleaned them into SQLite and asked what employers want. 73% of the postings don't say what they pay, and English is requested more often than any programming language.
