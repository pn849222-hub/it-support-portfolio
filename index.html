01 / Profile

A careful operator, not a ticket mill.

I provide hands-on IT support and system administration for servers, networks, databases, and virtualized environments. The work is practical: find the fault, change only what needs changing, test the result, and explain it clearly.

I like clearly defined technical problems — a server that will not boot, a VLAN that cannot talk, a database that needs a clean restore, a new environment built from scratch. I take time to understand the problem before I touch production.

Operating note

Understand the issue. Make careful changes. Test. Document what was done.

05 / Approach

How a job actually proceeds.

  1. Understand

    Collect symptoms, recent changes, and the expected state before changing anything.

  2. Change carefully

    Prefer reversible steps: snapshots, backups, one change at a time.

  3. Test

    Confirm the original issue is gone and that related services still work.

  4. Explain

    Leave a clear note of what failed, what was done, and what to watch next.
