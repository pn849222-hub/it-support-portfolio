01 / Profile

Backend and data, end to end.

I am a second-year Computer Science student in Ho Chi Minh City and a co-founder of VANG LAI, a community sports platform on .NET 10, PostgreSQL and Redis, with a Blazor web app and a React Native mobile app. I review and merge the code that goes into the main branch, and I ran its security and correctness pass: the admin token moved into an HttpOnly cookie, auth guards on the admin, health checks that report the real failure.

On my own I build data pipelines and the APIs that serve them. The part I care about is the part tutorials skip: real data arrives broken, and the job is to notice. One of my pipelines was silently dropping fifty jobs because an ID that looked unique was not. Finding that mattered more than any chart I drew afterwards.

Operating note

Understand the issue. Make careful changes. Test. Document what was done.

06 / Approach

How a job actually proceeds.

  1. Understand

    Collect symptoms, recent changes, and the expected state before changing anything.

  2. Change carefully

    Prefer reversible steps: snapshots, backups, one change at a time.

  3. Test

    Confirm the original issue is gone and that related services still work.

  4. Explain

    Leave a clear note of what failed, what was done, and what to watch next.
